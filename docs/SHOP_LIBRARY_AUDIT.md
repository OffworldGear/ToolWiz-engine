## What to fix first

1. **Delete or regenerate the old batch presets on the form tools.** The `CPM MagnaCut (Annealed ~22 HRC) - Adaptive Roughing / Roughing / Finishing / Slotting` presets on the three lollipop mills, the chamfer mill, the dovetail, the 3 mm spot drill and the three slot mills are end-mill strategies. On the 6 mm x 0.5 mm slot mill they carry a 23.95 mm stepdown (the cutter is 0.5 mm wide) and a 6 mm stepover (the neck leaves 1.5 mm of radial reach). Batch generation no longer writes these; for slot mills it writes `T-Slot Full Slot` presets (stepdown = kerf, stepover = radial depth per pass).
2. **Empty presets.** The 1/32" 4-flute `Default Preset` and `Stainless` presets have n = 0 and v_f = 0, and the 3/16" drill's presets store their feed only as plunge feed (that is how Fusion drill presets work, so those are fine).
3. **Chip loads more than 2 x ToolWiz** on the 3/8" bull nose (`Titanium - Roughing / Slotting`, the `SpeedTiger` variants) and the 1/32" tools (`Titanium Finishing`, `Titanium Slotting`); the 1/8" bull nose's Fusion stock `P 6-7 Steel Slot Milling` preset has f_z = 2 (a data entry error): re-generate them, or lower f_z to the ToolWiz value in the table.
4. **Speeds above 16,000 rpm** (Fusion's stock `P0-P5 ... Side Milling` presets on the 1/16" end mill, up to 35,141 rpm): the spindle cannot reach them; Fusion will program S above the machine limit.
5. **Holders.** Every tool in an ER collet holder whose required runout is in the precision / ultra class is flagged; the 1/32" and 1/16" tools need a shrink-fit, hydraulic or precision collet holder (see the finding for the required TIR).
6. **Measure runout** and record it (`toolwiz.runout_in`) on each tool; until then ToolWiz assumes 0.0002".
