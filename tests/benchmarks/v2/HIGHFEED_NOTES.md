# High-feed solid carbide end mill rows (staged)

Files: `highfeed_harvey.json` (260 rows), `highfeed_helical.json` (78), `highfeed_seco.json` (681: 100 SN400R + 135 SN200R from the 2017 Niagara catalog, 446 from Seco's own 2026.2 catalog: 320 JHF980 + 120 JHF181 + 6 SHF712). Harvey Tool and Helical Solutions are both Harvey Performance, so they count as one independent source; Seco/Niagara (one vendor group, Seco Tools) is a second. Collected 2026-11-12. `tool.end = "high_feed"`; extra tool/cut fields are listed in each file's `schema_extensions`. `tests/test_v2_benchmark_dataset.py` now accepts `end = "high_feed"` (corner_radius null or below DC/2).

## Harvey Tool: 260 rows
- Doc SF_709800, "Speeds & Feeds - High Feed End Mills for High Temp Alloys", https://harveyperformance.widen.net/content/dlwyepcnop/pdf/SF_709800.pdf (PDF created 2024-08-07, no printed revision). Series 7098xx, 7100xx, 7119xx-7124xx, 3x/5x/8x reach, Ti Nano coated. Geometry from https://harveytool.com/products/high-feed-end-mills-for-high-temp-alloys (table: LOC, reach multiple, neck, flutes, theoretical radius, shank, OAL).
- Diameters on the chart: .078, .093, .125, .187, .250 (4 flute up to 3/16, 5 flute at 1/4). Slot and Rgh chip load per diameter, one SFM per material row. Materials kept: carbon, low alloy, tool steel (including >50 HRC), 304/316 class, 400 series, PH (17-4), nickel alloy (Inconel 718 class), Ti alloy (Ti-6Al-4V), cobalt alloy. Skipped: specialty steels, cast irons, pure Ni, pure Ti. No aluminium row on this sheet.
- Depths are the sheet's rule table (slot: ap .03-.05 x D below 35 HRC, .025-.04 x D at 35 HRC and up, ae 1 x D; rough: ap .03-.05 / .0275-.0425 x D, ae >= .65 x D). `cut.ap` is the upper bound, `ap_min` the lower, rough `ae` is the minimum (`ae_is_minimum`). The "21-36 HRC" bands straddle the 35 HRC split; they use the "< 35 HRC" line. This is a derivation and is marked as such.
- Ambiguous: the sheet covers all three reach multiples with one set of numbers; rows use the 3x part. The column header "LENGTH OF CUT" on the catalog page is stored as LCF; the catalog gives no APMX, lead angle, helix, or center-cutting statement.

## Seco / Niagara Cutter SN400R: 100 rows
- Doc: Niagara Cutter "Solid Round Tooling" catalog and technical guide (PDF dated Oct-Dec 2017), https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0002/3468/4481/files/Niagara_Cutter_US_Catalog.pdf. This is a third-party-hosted copy of the manufacturer catalog, not Seco's own site. Part pages: PDF p.112 (series), p.115 (SN400R items, catalog printed p.113), p.117 and p.119 (4-flute slot and side start values; printed pages 115 and 117), p.18 (material classification).
- Data are start values by Seco Material Group (SMG): vc nominal with min-max, rpm, fz, feed rate, ap max. Slot ae 1.0 x D; side ae 0.30 x D. Mapping: SMG 1-2 to 1020, 3-4 to 1045, 5-6 to 4130-class, 7a (hardened) to H13, 8-9 and 10-11 to 316, 21 (Inconel 718) to High Temp Nickel Alloy, 22 (Ti 6Al-4V) to Titanium Alloy. The catalog has no PH stainless (17-4) group, so there are no 17-4 rows. Cast iron, Fe-based and Co-based superalloy groups and the 5-flute SN500R are not staged.
- Rows use the 3xD-reach 4-flute item (G1/E1) per diameter, 1/8 to 1/2. The 5xD and 7xD factors (APMX -20%/-40%, fz -15%/-30%) are in vendor_notes, not applied.
- Printed oddities: ap max "0.0012" for 1/4 in SMG 7a, 8-9, 10-11 (should be .0112 by the pattern) so those 1/4 cells are not staged. The side-milling table prints vc 459 as nominal for SMG 10-11 with a 262-361 range, inconsistent, so SMG 10-11 side rows are not staged. rpm x pi x D / 12 matches the printed vc on every staged row and fz x rpm x 4 matches vf within 4%.
- The tool's "LENGTH OF CUT" in the catalog table equals the ap max (e.g. .008 at 1/8), so LCF and apmx are both that value. The printed RADIUS is the defined radius rp (programming radius). The tool is a 0-degree helix, unequal index no, AlTiN, "-5 degree tapered" relief.

## Seco / Niagara Cutter SN200R: 135 rows
- Same catalog PDF as SN400R. Pages: PDF p.114 (SN200R item table, printed page 112), p.116 (2-flute slot start values, Zn = 2, printed 114), p.118 (2-flute side start values, printed 116), p.112 (series). Same material-group mapping, same fields, `tool.end = "high_feed"`, `flutes = 2`; rows use the 3xD-reach item (G1/E1) per diameter. Diameters 1/16, 3/32, 1/8, 5/32, 3/16, 1/4, 5/16, 3/8, 1/2 (N13984, N13987, N13992, N13997, N14004, N14009, N14016, N14023, N14029): per diameter 8 slot rows (SMG 1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7a, 8-9, 10-11, 21, 22) and 7 side rows (no SMG 10-11), 15 x 9 = 135. Per material at each diameter: 1020, 1045, 4130, H13, Ti one slot and one side row each; 316 two slot rows (SMG 8-9, 10-11) and one side row; Inconel 718 one slot and one side row.
- The SN200R tables print the 2-flute numbers: vc, rpm and fz as in the 4-flute table, vf exactly half (fz x rpm x 2). Printed vc matches rpm x pi x D / 12 within 0.5 % on all 135 rows; fz x rpm x 2 matches the printed vf within 1 % except 1/16 Inconel (5.5 %, fz is printed to 4 decimals: .0011). The 1/4 cells that carry the "0.0012" ap max typo in the 4-flute table print the correct .0112 here, so none is skipped; side SMG 10-11 is skipped for the same reason as the 4-flute table (vc 459 printed with a 262-361 range).
- Spot checks: 13 rows (random plus the 1/16 Ti and 316 slot rows) re-read from an independent text extraction of the PDF page; every rpm, fz, vf, ap max, vc nominal and range number appears in the page.
- Ambiguous: the G1 1/8 item (N13992) prints neck .082 (the 3/32 value) while G2/J3 and the SN400R 1/8 items print .109, so that row's neck_diameter is null (listed in `missing`). The 1/16 and 3/32 tools run at 60,157 and 40,105 rpm printed (1/16 in Ti 23,060 rpm, in 316 25,066 / 19,050 rpm): above a 16,000 rpm spindle. The catalog 'LENGTH OF CUT' again equals the printed ap max of the family (.004 at 1/16) while the chart ap max for S and 7a/M groups is lower (Ti .0020).
- 1/16 values: Ti-6Al-4V (SMG 22) slot 377 SFM (328-427), 23,060 rpm, fz .0011, ap .0020, 52 ipm; stainless SMG 8-9 410 SFM, 25,066 rpm, fz .0015, ap .0032, 75 ipm; SMG 10-11 312 SFM, 19,050 rpm, fz .0015, ap .0032, 57 ipm.

## Seco Solid End Mills catalog 2026.2: 446 rows (JHF980, JHF181, SHF712)
- Doc `SECO_SEM_2026_2`: Seco's own "GB-SolidEndMills-2026.2.pdf" (849 PDF pages; printed page = PDF page - 2), https://secotools.canto.global/pdfviewer/viewer/viewer.html?v=DownloadCenter&portalType=v%2FDownloadCenter&column=document&id=jplhn5nq090d3deligna3b8m6k&suffix=pdf . Read as text (`pdftotext -layout`), PDF page numbers (printed page in brackets). Cutting data are "target values" per Seco material group (SMG): ap/DCX (and ae/DCX for side), fz per DCX column (mm line, then inch line), vc m/min with sf/min and the min-max range, coolant code. Numeric fz and sf/min are the printed inch line (as in `seco.json`); the metric line is in `source.cells`.
- **Different SMG numbering than the Niagara rows.** The older Niagara catalog (2017) used its own "SMG" numbers (1-2, 3-4, 5-6, 7a, 8-9, 10-11, 21 Inconel 718, 22 Ti-6Al-4V). Seco's 2026.2 catalog uses the ISO-style groups P1-P12, M1-M5, K1-K7, S1-S3, S11-S13, H3-H31 (PDF p.836-838, printed 834-836). The two numberings are unrelated (Niagara "22" = Ti-6Al-4V; Seco 2026.2 "S12" = Ti-6Al-4V), so rows of the two catalogs are mapped separately to the same ToolWiz families.
- **JHF980** (high feed, universal, 2-5 flutes, DCX 1-12 mm): item tables PDF p.232-233 (printed 230-231), side milling p.234 (232), slot milling p.235 (233). 320 rows = 20 item sizes x 8 SMG x 2 operations. Sizes (representative = shortest reach LN for that DCX and flute count): 1.5 mm 2FL; 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 mm 2FL and 4FL; 8, 10, 12 mm 2FL, 3FL, 5FL. Items: 980015-MEGA 02511199 (1.5 mm), 980030-MEGA 02511224 and JHF980030G2H.0Z4-MEGA 03003387 (3 mm 2FL / 4FL), and so on (the item and designation are in each row). Grade/coating MEGA. 1.0 mm is not staged (0.039 in is below the 1/16"-1/2" range). ae = 0.30 x DCX side, DCX slot; ap/DCX 0.040 (P, 0.036 M/H, 0.022 S) so ap never reaches APMXS (0.2 mm at 3 mm; no cap needed on any row).
- **JHF181** (high feed, hardened steel, 3-5 flutes, DCX 2-16 mm): items PDF p.535 (533), side milling roughing p.536 (534), slot milling p.537 (535). 120 rows = 15 item sizes x 4 SMG x 2 operations (H8, H12, S3, S12). Sizes: 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 mm 3FL and 4FL; 10 mm 3FL, 4FL, 5FL; 12 mm 4FL, 5FL. 16 mm (0.63 in) is above the 1/2" range and not staged. Grade HXT. Side milling: ae/DCX 0.30 (P, K, H) or 0.18 (S).
- **SHF712** (high feed, ISO S, 2 flutes, DCX 3/4/6 mm, grade HXT): items PDF p.435 (433), side + slot p.436 (434). 6 rows (S12 = Ti-6Al-4V slot and side at 3, 4, 6 mm; items SHF712030G3R050.0Z2 10106495, SHF712040G2R050.0Z2 10106493, SHF712060E2R050.0Z2 10106494). Its other group, S2 (cobalt-based superalloys), is not mapped (see below). It prints no S3 (Inconel) group and no 17-4 group.
- **SME714/716 (HFM, side roughing only): not staged.** The page is titled "Miniature - ISO-S - Square - 2-3 Flutes - Sharp or corner radius" (DC 0.5-2 mm, RE 0 or 0.05-0.1 mm, ap/DC 0.07, ae/DC 0.08): a square miniature end mill with a small corner radius, not a high-feed front geometry like the others (no APMXS-defined radius profile, no RP, no lead). Its data are ae 0.08 x DC at fz about 0.002-0.014 mm, a finishing-class cut, so they would be mislabelled as high-feed.
- Geometry (item tables): DCX = max cutting diameter = `tool.DC` (all Seco cutting-data tables and "ae/ap x DCX" use DCX); Seco's printed DC (diameter at APMXS) is `tool.DC_at_apmx_mm`. APMXS = `tool.apmx` and `LCF`; RE = `corner_radius`; RP (JHF980 only) = `programming_radius`; DN = neck_diameter; LN = LB; DMM = SFDM. The printed PSIR column (JHF980 -5.0, SHF712 1.5) is stored in `tool.psir_deg_printed` and NOT used as `lead_angle_deg` (the engine would read a positive value as the lead angle); helix, variable pitch, centre cutting and max ramp are not printed (listed in `missing`). JHF181 and SHF712 print no RP, so `programming_radius` is null for those rows (listed in `missing`).
- ap = ap/DCX x DCX (mm) converted to inch, never above APMXS: no row exceeded APMXS (the largest ap factor, 0.040 x DCX, is just under the smallest APMXS/DCX, 0.0417 for JHF980 at 12 mm: 0.48 mm vs 0.5 mm; at 3 mm 0.12 mm vs 0.2 mm).

### Checks and printed inconsistencies
- Every row's inch/metric pair was compared by script: fz mm / 25.4 vs printed in/tooth within 3.1 % (the printed inch values are rounded to 2 significant digits); vc m/min x 3.2808 vs printed sf/min within 4 % for nominal and range on every row except SMG P2.
- **Catalog inconsistency, JHF980 P2 (40 rows, noted in each row):** the printed sf/min range does not equal the printed m/min range (slot vc 330 (300-360) m/min prints 1075 (990-1100) sf/min, but 360 m/min = 1181 sf/min; side 375 (340-410) prints 1225 (1200-1300), 410 m/min = 1345). The nominal values agree. Rows keep the printed inch line; the metric line is in `source.cells`.
- Spot checks: 14 random rows plus the Ti and 17-4 3 mm and 1.5 mm slot rows re-read against the text dump of the page (column by column: DCX header, ap/DCX, fz mm and inch, vc and range, sf/min and range); all agree. The extraction parses the page by SMG anchor, so every row is checked structurally (10 columns for JHF980, 8 for JHF181, 3 for SHF712 on all SMG lines).
- The page title of SHF712's cutting data reads "SFH712" in the catalog (typo, same series).

## SN400R question
The part is real. SN400R is the Niagara Cutter (a Seco Tools company) 4-flute solid carbide high-feed end mill with AlTiN coating, defined radius rp, 0-degree helix, 1/8 to 1/2 diameter, 3x/5x reach. The owner's item "SN400R-0.125-G1-H015.0-Z4" (order N13995, Seco item 03043584, 1/8 dia, 1/4 shank, 2 in OAL, .375 reach, neck .109, radius .0148) exists, and G2 (N13996, 2-1/2 OAL, .625 reach) too. Marketing says "focus on ISO P, S and K materials including stainless steel, Inconel and titanium"; in the data it is a shallow-ap (ap max .008 at 1/8), high-feed start-value tool, not a Seco "Jabro" part. The `.125` in the owner's wording is the diameter in the part code. High-temp-alloy start values: Inconel 718 98 sfm (66-131); Ti-6Al-4V 377 sfm (328-427).
