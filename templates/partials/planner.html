Strategy Planner

Which strategy cuts this feature fastest without wearing the tool out much faster? ToolWiz compares a full-width contour slot, a smaller tool and adaptive clearing for a cutout or slot, or compares T-slot cutters, using its own speeds, feeds and depths for every option.

How the recommendation is chosen (wear-ratio rule)

Every option gets a wear per part number: minutes the edge is in the cut divided by the tool life at the speed ToolWiz chose (Taylor law, relative to the 50 % baseline speed). Wear is scaled so the option that wears the tool least is 1.00; only the ratios mean anything.

The recommendation is the fastest option whose wear is within a limit of the least-wear option. The limit rises with aggressiveness: 1.1× at 0 %, 1.3× at 50 %, 2.0× at 100 %. So at 50 % you accept up to 30 % more tool wear per part to save cycle time, but not a tool that wears several times faster. Tool life rests on a placeholder Taylor exponent: treat big wear ratios as direction, not as a prediction.
